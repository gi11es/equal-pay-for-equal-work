# Compensation-policy review — October 30, 2026

This review covers all 24 organizations in the previous README and a wider search for additions. The resulting main list has 24 organizations: 17 retained, seven added, two retired for contradictory current evidence, and five moved to “Needs current verification.”

The distinction matters: an unsubstantiated policy is not a proven policy reversal. No organization was removed solely for having no vacancies, and an acquisition alone was not treated as evidence of a compensation change.

## Method

- Read the existing list and repository history, including earlier removals, before considering additions.
- Check company careers pages, handbooks, compensation statements, and employer-controlled job boards. Use third-party lists as leads, not proof.
- Prefer live first-party policies over old announcements. Compare current job descriptions with the claimed policy and check hiring restrictions separately.
- Check original URLs with HTTP GET and follow redirects. Inspect content as well as status: a successful response, a JavaScript shell, a pay range, or an empty job board does not establish a compensation policy.
- Inspect public Greenhouse and Ashby job feeds where available, and employer page data where feeds are incomplete. Do not infer a geographic pay difference merely from different currencies.

This is a review of published evidence, not a payroll audit. A review date records when a source was checked; it does not imply that the company updated its policy that day. Restrictions and vacancies can change after this review.
