# Equal pay for equal work

Organizations that pay remote workers using location-independent salaries or salary ranges.

**Last reviewed: October 30, 2026.** See the [review notes](audits/2026-10-30.md) for the evidence, removals, link repairs, and companies considered.

## What qualifies

An organization must publicly state that location does not determine pay for comparable work, or advertise a fixed salary for a role open across multiple locations. Salary can still vary with role, level, experience, or performance. A published salary range or a remote-work policy alone is not enough.

Country and time-zone hiring restrictions are listed separately: a company hiring only within one country can qualify, but a multinational using different country or regional pay bands does not. A policy covering only certain roles is labelled accordingly. Benefits, taxes, currency conversion, and take-home pay can differ.

Sources are company policies, handbooks, or employer-controlled job postings wherever available. Older announcements are identified as such. An accessible page is evidence of a published policy, not an independent payroll audit. Hiring links do not imply that positions are currently open.

## Contributing

Please include an explicit employer source, the roles and countries the policy covers, any geographic or time-zone restrictions, and the date you checked it. Check current job postings for contradictory geographic pay language. Salary transparency, a worldwide team, or a San Francisco benchmark alone does not prove location-independent pay.
